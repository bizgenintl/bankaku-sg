Signature

Yukari — Original Prawn Cracker

〈ゆかり〉

Bankaku's flagship product, crafted since 1889. Each cracker is made with approximately 70% natural prawn, slow-dried and baked using a traditional technique passed down from the Edo period. Crisp, fragrant, and deeply savoury — a timeless taste that has delighted generations across Japan and beyond.

Saku Saku Nikki

〈さくさく日記〉

"Saku Saku" — the Japanese word for that satisfying crisp crunch. Thinner and lighter than the classic Yukari, these bite-sized crackers are perfect for everyday snacking. Comes in cheerful, gift-friendly packaging that brings a smile with every box.

坂角総本舗について

Founded in 1889 in Yokosuka, Aichi Prefecture, BANKAKU has spent over 130 years crafting prawn crackers that honour the natural gifts of the sea. Our flagship YUKARI traces its roots to the Edo era, when prawn delicacies were presented to the Tokugawa Shogunate. Today, we bring that same tradition and heartfelt craftsmanship to Singapore.

Est. 1889 · Nagoya, Japan
